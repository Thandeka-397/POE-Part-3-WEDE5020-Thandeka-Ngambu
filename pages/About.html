About Our Organisation

Empowering youth and transforming communities through quality education, talent development, and resource support in Gqeberha.

📜

Brief History

Edu-Motherwell Development Organisation was founded in 2008 in Gqeberha. This organisation assists at the community level to address youth unemployment and low educational resources in our local communities.

🎯

Mission Statement

To assist youth in finding and developing their talents, realizing their potential in life, and creating opportunities for impoverished youth to access quality education.

👁️

Vision Statement

To overflow South African youth communities with local graduates who break the cycle of poverty, serve as role models for future generations, and drive community transformation.

Edu-Motherwell

Providing educational support, community upliftment, and development resources to empower vulnerable groups in Motherwell, Gqeberha.
